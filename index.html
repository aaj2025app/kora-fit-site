Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 16, 2026

Kora Fit ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data within the Kora Fit mobile application and website.

1. Data Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us during the onboarding process, including age, weight, fitness goals, and dietary preferences. If you use our AI Calorie Tracker, we process the images you upload to identify food items and estimate nutritional content.

2. Camera & Photo Access

Kora Fit requires access to your device camera or photo library specifically for the AI food tracking feature. Images are used solely for nutritional analysis and are not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Use of Data

We use your data to:

  • Generate custom workout plans based on your profile.
  • Provide personalized meal recommendations.
  • Track your caloric intake via AI image recognition.
  • Improve the accuracy of our AI health models.

4. Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell your personal or health data. Data is stored securely and is only used to provide the services requested within the app.

5. Your Rights

You may request to delete your account and all associated health data at any time through the app settings or by contacting our support team.
